A collaborative AI code-review council that combines multiple specialized agents into one actionable engineering verdict.

- Run parallel reviewers for correctness, security, performance, testing, and maintainability.
- Aggregate findings, remove duplicates, and rank issues by severity and confidence.
- Require agents to cite exact files, lines, and reproducible reasoning.
- Provide a final consensus report with suggested patches and unresolved disagreements.
- Support local repositories, configurable review policies, and CI integration.
